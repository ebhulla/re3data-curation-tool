## Method note (also relevant for readers)
Pair labels used for training and evaluation were produced by an LLM judge (gpt-oss-120b via Groq), not by people.
It was checked against 30 hand-labeled pairs and a random-pair sanity check (see `docs/results_2026-10-14.md`).
