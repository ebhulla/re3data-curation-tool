# Preliminary results, 2026-10-14

Task: decide whether two institution names are the SAME institution (judge label `duplicate`) or not
(`hierarchy` or `unrelated`). Labels come from the swap-checked LLM judge (Groq, gpt-oss-120b). Pairs where the two
orderings disagreed, or the judge said `uncertain`, are dropped.

Data: 2,290 judged pairs (600 random/ROR-based + 1,690 mined by embedding nearest neighbors), 1,591 usable after the
leakage-safe split (`split_pairs.py`: no institution name or ROR cluster appears in two splits).
Threshold for each method is chosen on train, applied unchanged to test.

| Split | Pairs | Duplicates |
|---|---|---|
| train | 1,459 | 270 |
| val | 50 | 18 |
| test | 82 | 23 |

## Test results (82 pairs, 23 true duplicates)

| Method | Precision | Recall | F1 | AP | mean score: duplicate | hierarchy | unrelated |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| exact (normalized equality) | 1.00 | 0.39 | 0.56 | 0.56 | 0.39 | 0.00 | 0.00 |
| substring (naive rule) | 0.41 | 0.61 | 0.49 | 0.36 | 0.61 | 0.69 | 0.00 |
| fuzzy (rapidfuzz token_sort) | 0.89 | 0.74 | 0.81 | 0.94 | 0.93 | 0.70 | 0.54 |
| MiniLM, off the shelf | 0.77 | 0.87 | 0.82 | 0.91 | 0.96 | 0.78 | 0.56 |
| MiniLM, fine-tuned (1 seed) | 0.71 | 0.87 | 0.78 | 0.88 | 0.94 | 0.69 | 0.43 |

## What the numbers support
- The substring rule cannot separate duplicates from hierarchies: it scores hierarchy pairs (0.69) above duplicates (0.61).
- Fuzzy matching and off-the-shelf MiniLM are close (F1 0.81 vs 0.82); fuzzy has the higher AP (0.94 vs 0.91).
- Fine-tuning (one seed) widened the average gap between duplicates and hierarchies (0.25 vs 0.18) but did not improve
  F1 or AP on the test set.

## What they do not support
- Any claim that fine-tuning helps or hurts: the test set has 23 duplicates, so one pair moves recall by about 4 points,
  and the differences above (0.03 to 0.04 F1) are within that noise. The six-run sweep is meant to give error bars.
- Any claim about hard duplicates (acronyms, translations): mined pairs were selected by MiniLM similarity of 0.60 or
  higher, which favors near-identical names, and duplicates with very different strings are probably under-sampled.
- Judge accuracy beyond the 30 hand-labeled pairs (29 of 29 swap-consistent matched) and the random-pair check
  (98% of 200 random pairs labeled unrelated).
